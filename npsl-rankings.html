NPSL rankings — common questions

Does NPSL publish a national ranking?
No. NPSL publishes division and conference standings. Ranked XI builds the national table by rating all 46 rated NPSL clubs on one scale that also covers MLS, USL, NPSL and college soccer.
How are NPSL clubs rated?
Ratings from a match-by-match Elo walk over every scored 2026 NPSL result — backtested and calibrated (Brier 0.531 vs 0.667 uniform baseline). Cross-league placement is calibrated on roughly 600 U.S. Open Cup results.
Can a NPSL club rate above a professional club?
Yes, and some do. The scale is calibrated on Open Cup results, where amateur sides genuinely beat professional ones, so the table reflects that rather than assuming a league hierarchy.
