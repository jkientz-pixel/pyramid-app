Every club in American soccer.
One map. One table.

Ranked XI is the national table of American soccer: every club from MLS to the grassroots, men's and women's, ranked on one scale. 4,367 clubs mapped · 2,093 men's and 889 women's rated — with real pro rosters and stats, club honours, player pages and the pathway between every level.
