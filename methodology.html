How the ratings are built

League results feed a weekly Elo rating per club. Cup competitions — U.S. Open Cup qualifying, the National Amateur Cup — are where leagues actually meet, and roughly 600 cross-league Cup results from the last five editions calibrate the gaps between levels. Every rating labels its basis on the page where it appears: real results, real standings, an independent results model, or an illustrative placeholder until that league's feed connects. Fixtures and results are never invented.

What "independent results model" means. It is the most common basis label on the site, so it deserves its own definition. The wins and losses underneath these ratings are real — every scored game a team played that season — but the rating math was run by an independent third party (primarily Massey Ratings) rather than by our own Elo engine. We take that independent ordering, check its coverage against our club list, and map it onto the national scale using the calibrated bands described below. It is one step less direct than match-by-match Elo we compute ourselves, which is exactly why it carries its own label instead of borrowing real results.

College is placed, not measured. Varsity teams never play in the Open Cup, so no real cross-league results anchor them. College layers use independent Massey ratings (season snapshots) for order within each division, mapped onto the national scale in calibrated bands. Men's bands top out just below the professional USL Championship floor — a top college side rates like a strong USL League One / League Two team, whose summer rosters are largely these same players (recalibrated Aug 2026; earlier snapshots ran higher). Women's college bands sit deliberately higher: college is the women's game's primary development tier, so the top D1 programs rate just below the NWSL floor.

The four basis labels

Every rating on Ranked XI carries one of these four labels, on the page where it appears. The label is the honest part: it says how directly the number was measured.

LabelWhat the number is built fromHow direct
Real resultsElo walked match-by-match over every scored fixture the club played this season.Most direct — we compute it from the games.
Real standingsThe division publishes a table but not usable match results, so the rating is fitted to points and goal difference.Direct, but one step removed from the individual games.
Independent results modelReal games, but the rating math was run by a third party (primarily Massey Ratings) and mapped onto our scale.Real evidence, someone else's arithmetic.
IllustrativeNo usable feed is connected for this competition yet. Not a measurement. It demonstrates the product and says so.

How Ranked XI compares

Club Coefficient scores men's clubs by multi-year honors and league strength. Ranked XI puts men's and women's, college included, on one current Elo-style scale, and labels whether each rating is real results, real standings, an independent model, or illustrative.

ESPN, MLSSoccer, FotMob, Sofascore and Transfermarkt all rank clubs inside a league. Wikipedia explains the American league system as a diagram. None of them answer the question this site exists for: where does a given club sit against every other club in the country, on one number, with the basis of that number stated. That table is at the US soccer pyramid, ranked.

Privacy

No tracking cookies, no analytics identifiers, no advertising pixels. Favorites and follows live in your browser's storage and are sent to us only if you choose to save your XI to an email address, which is optional and off by default. If you join the list or send a request through a form, we store what you submit — your name, contact details, and the request — so we can reply and let you know when things ship. That list is never sold or shared, and one email to hello@rankedxi.com removes you. The full policy lives at rankedxi.com/privacy. Corrections and removal requests: Terms, Privacy & Notices.
