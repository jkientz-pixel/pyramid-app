Every club Ranked XI maps: pro, amateur, college, and youth, men's and women's sides both — including 1,300+ women's clubs from the NWSL down through WPSL and UWS.

Who this is for

Football Manager database creators asked first: the club list, real league structure, and real geography are the slow part of building a US pyramid mod, and that part is done here. It works just as well for maps, research, or any project that needs to know what actually exists at every level of American soccer. The competition rules — the part that makes a US save feel like US soccer instead of a pro/rel clone — are still yours to build.

What's in each row

FieldMeaning

genderM or W
city, state, lat, lonHome city and coordinates; location_accuracy says verified or approximate
ratingElo-style rating on the snapshot date. Blank means unrated — youth listings never carry ratings
rating_basisHow the rating was built: real_results, standings, or results_model — full detail on the methodology page
website, crest_urlClub's own site, and the crest as shown on Ranked XI for identification

What's not in here, and why

No rosters and no crest image pack. Roster data reaches us through league feeds with their own terms, and crest artwork belongs to the clubs — neither is ours to redistribute in bulk. Youth clubs are organization listings only: no ratings, no player data, ever.
