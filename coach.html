What gets you up the rankings?

Pick yourself — any player with 450+ minutes in MLS, NWSL, USL Championship, USL League One, MLS Next Pro or USL Super League. Your rating is built from per-96 numbers, weighted for your position and compared against your league. Below it: the training plan — drag a scroller to simulate improvement, or tap a target to apply the realistic half-step, and watch the names you'd pass.

Pro leagues only for now. Amateur and youth players don't publish per-player match stats, so they can't be rated yet — that changes when clubs connect their own data.

What that buys you

Data: American Soccer Analysis public API — MLS, NWSL, USL Championship, USL League One, MLS Next Pro (2026) and USL Super League (2025-26), players with 450+ minutes, goalkeepers excluded (their levers are save data we don't ingest yet). Ratings and percentiles compare you to position peers in your own league. The 0–99 rating is experimental: position-weighted z-scores over per-96 stats including goals-added (g+) value. Amateur and youth leagues aren't covered — no public per-player stats exist for them yet. Nothing you simulate here is saved. rankedxi.com · methodology & disclaimer · privacy
