Privacy Policy

Applies to rankedxi.com and the Ranked XI app (including the Android app, which is this website in an app shell). Material changes are dated here. Last updated August 2, 2026.

The short version

No accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies, no third-party trackers, and nothing is ever sold or shared. We count pageviews ourselves, without recording who you are. The only personal information Ranked XI holds is what you type into a signup or request form — and one email removes it.

What stays on your device

Your preferences and activity live in your browser's own storage and never leave your device:

  • Follows and favorites — the clubs you follow are kept in local browser storage (IndexedDB) so your feed works. We have no copy. Following a club sends us nothing; it is only if you separately type your email into the club-results form that we hold an address at all.
  • Display settings — theme and layout choices are kept in local storage.
  • Offline cache — a service worker caches app files on your device so the app loads fast and works offline.

Clearing the site's data in your browser (or uninstalling the app) removes all of it.

What we store when you submit a form

If you join the updates list or send a request through a form, we store exactly what the form sends so we can reply and let you know when things ship: the date and time, the kind of request, and — as provided by you — your name, email address, role, state, and message, plus which page the form was on and your browser's user-agent string (spam control). This lives in a private database with access limited to the project owner. It is used only to respond to you and send the updates you asked for. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

One of those forms is the club-results email: if you ask for a club's results by email we store your address, that club's name, the date, your confirmation that you are 13 or older, and your user-agent. It is used to send you that club's results and nothing else. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing is honored immediately.

Age. The club-results email is for people 13 and older, and we ask you to confirm that before we store anything. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us an address, write to us and it comes out same-day.

To be removed or to see what we hold for your email address, write to hello@rankedxi.com — removal is honored within 7 days, no questions asked.

How we count visits

We need to know roughly how many people use the site and which pages they open. That counting runs on our own servers. No analytics company is involved and the site loads no script from anyone else's domain.

For each pageview we store the page you opened, the website that linked you here (just the site name, never the full address), a broad device category such as "iPhone" or "Windows", and your country. We also store two random strings your browser makes up for itself: one that lets us tell a repeat visit from a first visit, and one that groups the pages of a single visit together.

Those random strings are not derived from anything about you, they are not shared with anyone, and they cannot identify you or follow you to another website. Clearing this site's data in your browser erases them permanently.

We do not store your IP address, your full browser user-agent, or the full address of the page that linked you here. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we count nothing at all.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in — so there is nothing to breach and nothing to delete beyond the form list above.
  • No third-party analytics, ad networks, or social trackers. No data is shared with third parties.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking of any kind. Nothing we store can follow you to another website.
  • No location access. The map shows club locations, never yours.
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts access.

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard server logs — IP address and request metadata — to serve and secure the site, as every website's host does. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.

Children and minors

Ranked XI is a reference site, not a social product, and collects no personal information from anyone unless they submit a form. Our data policy for youth soccer content is stricter than the law requires: youth clubs appear as organization listings only, with no ratings and no player data, and we never publish personal information about minors.
