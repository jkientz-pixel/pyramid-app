UPSL rankings — common questions

Does UPSL publish a national ranking?
No. UPSL publishes division and conference standings. Ranked XI builds the national table by rating all 645 rated UPSL clubs on one scale that also covers MLS, USL, NPSL and college soccer.
How are UPSL clubs rated?
Ratings from real division standings (points + goal difference), updated as tables move. Cross-league placement is calibrated on roughly 600 U.S. Open Cup results.
Can a UPSL club rate above a professional club?
Yes, and some do. The scale is calibrated on Open Cup results, where amateur sides genuinely beat professional ones, so the table reflects that rather than assuming a league hierarchy.
